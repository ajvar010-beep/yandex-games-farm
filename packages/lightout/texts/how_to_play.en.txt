Tap a tile on the board: it flips together with its four neighbours, left, right, above and below. That cross is the whole rule.

Clear every light before the moves run out. Each tap spends one move, and the counter sits in the top panel.

Stuck? Open the shop: a hint clears one light of the solution, undo takes back the last press and its move, and three moves add room to spare.

Cleared levels pay coins, and a frugal run with spare moves and no hints pays more.
