Drag a finger along a straight line of neighbouring letters to spell a word from the list. Lift your finger: a correct word is highlighted on the grid and crossed off the list. Find every word before the timer runs out. Spend the coins you earn in the shop: a hint flashes the first letter of a word, shuffle lays the remaining words out again, and +30 seconds adds time to the clock. The pause button is in the top right corner.
