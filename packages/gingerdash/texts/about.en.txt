Ginger Dash is a cheerful runner about a ginger cat on a meadow. The cat runs on its own — your job is to tap in time to jump over boxes, puddles and birds. The farther you run, the faster the cat goes and the more valuable the fish along the track become. A long no-crash streak multiplies your score up to ×5, and each level pays out fish — the store currency. Spend them on a fish magnet, a soft shield that absorbs one hit, cat calm that slows time down, or an extra life. Keep your eyes open: sometimes a golden fish flies by high above — it takes a double jump to reach, but it holds 5× more points! Complete all 20 levels, reach the finish and set a distance record. Simple one-tap controls, warm hand-drawn look, cloud saves and best scores — play on your phone or computer.
