Tap the screen (or press Space) to rotate the pizza by one slice. A falling topping must land on the glowing golden slice — that scores points and builds your combo. A miss leaves a stain and costs a life; you have two. Catch the required number of toppings to reach the next level. From level 3 watch for golden star-toppings — they give ×5 score. In the shop you can spend crumbs on a napkin (absorbs one miss), a warm oven (slows the fall), a golden crumb (double score) and a wide slice (doubles the golden zone).
