Hexwave is an arcade core-defense game. Five enemy types and a huge warden dive at your core from every direction — watch for the blue lancers and their sudden dash! Orbit the guardian — it fires by itself; your job is to aim it and time the pulse blast. Between waves spend coins on seven upgrades: damage, fire rate, volleys, core integrity, pulse, a stasis field and the Aegis shield that blocks the first hit on the core each wave. Difficulty rises every wave, bosses every tenth. Records and progress sync to the cloud; guest play works too.
