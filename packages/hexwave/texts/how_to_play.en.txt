Phone: drag your finger around the core — the guardian follows the orbit and fires automatically. Quick tap on the field or the bottom-right button — pulse blast. Pause button is top-right. Desktop: hold the left mouse button and drag around the core, or press A/D (arrow keys) to rotate; Space for pulse, P/Esc to pause. Buy upgrades between waves: from wave 5 beware the blue lancers — they suddenly dash at the core, and the Aegis shield blocks the first hit each wave. Watch a video to revive the core or earn coins.
