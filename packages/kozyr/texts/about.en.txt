Trump Card is Russian Durak against twelve tavern regulars. Every rival plays their own way: Tikhon panics and takes cards early, Kuzma piles on attacks greedily, Agrafena saves her trumps to the very end, and Akim the Trump counts every card that has been played.

Beat a card with a higher card of the same suit or with a trump, add only ranks that are already on the table, say "done" in time, or take the whole pile. Whoever runs out of cards first once the deck is empty wins, and the loser stays the fool.

Wins pay coins: the more cards left in your rival's hand, the bigger the reward, and a game where you never take a single card pays a bonus. Coins go into three perks: a hint that highlights the cards you can beat with, a one-time swap of a bad hand, and a short peek at your rival's cards. The hint is bought once, swap and peek are charges, and a stake raises the payout.

Twelve rivals unlock one after another, each tougher. Reach Akim and prove the trump is you.
