1. Tap a cell on the fortress, pick a block or a weapon and place it.
2. Mind the rows: saw and shocker work in rows 1–2, flamethrower in 2–4, laser in 3–5, machine gun in 4–6, grenade launcher in 5–6. A weapon in the wrong row does not fire.
3. Every weapon has its own range: the saw shreds at the wall, the machine gun reaches eight cells.
4. Between waves repair the hull, add blocks and upgrade weapons — up to three levels.
5. Press Fight! and the fortress rolls on its own. Reach the boss at the end of the street and bring it down.
