Hacker Parkour is a dynamic 3D platformer inside a neon MATRIX. Run and jump across glowing platforms, dodge data-lava, collect cyber coins. All 9 levels are a hardcore hack: serpentines, spike gauntlets, crumbling floors, moving platforms, vertical shafts and ice. Checkpoints save your progress, but there's no mercy. Upgrades, skins (including the neon Hacker), time leaderboard. Finish faster to earn stars. Hack all 27 stars!
