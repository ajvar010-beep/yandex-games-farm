The cat runs on its own — tap the screen (or press Space) to make it jump over a box, a puddle or a bird. Tap again in mid-air to double-jump: that is how you reach the golden fish, which holds 5× more points. A crash costs a life — you start with two, but the store sells an extra one. Fish on the track are both score and store currency: collect them without losing pace. A streak of six or more fish without a fall enables a ×2..×5 multiplier. Cover the target distance to advance to the next level — there are 20, and the speed keeps rising. The ★ button opens the store: the fish magnet attracts fish, the soft shield forgives one hit, cat calm slows time down, and the Nine Lives item grants another life. If you run out of lives, once per run you can watch a video and continue from the same spot. Run as far as you can!
