Tap the screen when the swinging layer is right above the tower — it will drop onto the top. Any overhang is cut off: the layer gets thinner and the game speeds up. Landing exactly in the center counts as perfect, keeps the layer full-width and builds a combo multiplier up to ×5. The rare star layer gives a whole ×15 points — don't miss it! Stack the required number of layers to advance to the next level — there are 20 in total. Levels and accuracy earn you sprinkles: spend them at the bakery (the ★ button) on a guide ruler, a pendulum slowdown, layer widening and bouncy cream that keeps layers from being cut at all. If a layer misses the tower, it collapses — but once per run you can watch a video and continue from the same spot. Set height records!
