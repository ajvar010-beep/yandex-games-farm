Tap the bubbles rising from the seabed to pop them! Blue bubbles give points, pearl bubbles give 5× more, and the rare jellyfish gives a whole ×15! Hearts restore a life, capsules trigger bonuses. Do NOT touch the mines — they explode and cost a life! A no-miss streak grows the score multiplier up to ×5. Complete the level goal to climb higher. Spend pearls ❁ in the shop (the star button) on slow time, double pearls, the protective net and the tide that drags all bubbles down.
