Word Grid is a calm word puzzle for anyone who likes letters. Every level hides a set of themed words in a letter grid: drag a finger across neighbouring cells and the word forms under your hand. Found words are highlighted on the grid and crossed off the list. There are 12 themes — sea, forest, space, kitchen, sport, music, garden, city, winter, school, party and travel — and 12 levels, with the grid growing from 8×8 to 12×12 as the words get longer. Each level has a timer, coins for every word and a small shop with a hint, a reshuffle and extra time. Play in your browser on phone or desktop, in Russian or English: your progress and best score are saved.
