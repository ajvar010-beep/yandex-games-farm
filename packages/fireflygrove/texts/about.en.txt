Firefly Grove is a cozy night arcade about reflexes. Fireflies wander across the twilight meadow and flash with warm light for a moment. Tap a glowing firefly to catch it and gather light dust. A timely catch brings points and a growing combo up to ×5; tapping a dark one scares it off, and a crawling caterpillar's sting costs a whole life — you have two.

Each level brings more fireflies — they fly faster and flash shorter. From level 5 a rare moon moth appears: it glows all the time and pays ×4 score, but flies faster than the rest. In the store you can spend dust on a bubble (absorbs one sting), a lantern (fireflies glow longer), a net (double catch radius) and a brew (caterpillars crawl at half speed). Clear all 20 levels, return to the grove by watching a video, and set a record that is kept in the cloud.
