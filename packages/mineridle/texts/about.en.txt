Miner Clicker is an idle mining game in blocky style. Tap the rock — it cracks and drops coins. Spend coins on gear you can SEE working on screen: pickaxes swing above the rock, miners in glowing helmets dig beside it, drills and lasers bite into stone.

30 levels of depth — from a warm mine to the magma core. Every 5th level is a boss slab on a timer. Special levels change the rules: machines can't work in flooded shafts, the golden pickaxe is banned on "honest work". New gear unlocks every 1–3 levels.

Catch golden veins with crystals, upgrade your hit and offline crew, claim the daily reward, compete for depth on the leaderboard. English and Russian supported, progress saved to the cloud.
