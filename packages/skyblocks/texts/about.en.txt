Sky Blocks is a cozy idle clicker about floating islands in the night sky. Tap the fluffy clouds to gather crystals — and hunt the rare golden cloud, which holds eight times more! Unlock new islands — from the humble Grove to the Sky Palace — and upgrade them so crystals pour in by themselves. The shop offers tap power, a cloud magnet, a storm with triple income, and Harvest, which instantly collects crystals from every island at once. Complete goals, level up and assemble the whole archipelago. The game saves automatically, and with Yandex ID your progress is kept in the cloud on any device.
