1. Balls drop from the hopper one at a time. Each one bounces off the pins and lands in one of three cups.
2. Tap a pin to pop it out — the next balls will take a different path.
3. Check what each cup needs: its window shows how many balls are inside and how many are required.
4. Fill all three cups to clear the level. If you run out of balls, restart or watch an ad for extra balls.
5. Spend coins in the workshop on ball stock, a brake and pliers.
