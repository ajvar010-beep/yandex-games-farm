Block Fit is an engaging puzzle in the spirit of Tetris and block puzzles. Drag shapes from the panel onto the 10×10 board. Filled rows and columns clear and earn points. The level goal is to reach the target score.

20 levels with growing difficulty. Winning earns coins that can be spent in the shop on boosters: best place hint, new shapes, undo placement, or a bomb that clears a 3×3 area.

Come back every day — consecutive day streaks earn bonus coins. Compete with yourself: what level can you reach?
