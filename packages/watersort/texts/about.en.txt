Aqualine is a cozy logic puzzle about water and color. A row of tubes holds layers of colored liquid: in one move you may pour the top layer into an empty tube or onto a layer of the same color. The goal looks simple and turns out tricky — sort every tube into a single color without tangling yourself up. Each level adds more colors and fewer empty tubes, so you have to plan several moves ahead.

Cleared levels earn you coins to spend in the shop on an undo, an extra tube, a hint or a shuffle. Twenty levels, cozy underwater visuals and cloud saves — start on your phone and continue on your computer.
