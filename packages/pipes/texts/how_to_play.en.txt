1. Tap a copper pipe to turn it 90 degrees clockwise.
2. Join the tap on the left to the drain on the right: the water must cross the whole chain.
3. Every turn costs a move. Run out of moves and the level fails.
4. The more moves you save, the more coins you earn.
5. A shop hint turns one wrong pipe the right way and costs no move.
6. A fifteen-second freeze lets you turn pipes for free.
7. From level nine there are two taps: a junction must link both routes to the drain.
