Merge Sweets is a sweet physics puzzle. Drop glossy sweets into the cup: matching ones merge on contact into bigger ones, chaining from a tiny candy up to the legendary SOUFFLÉ. The bigger the sweet, the more coins and points.

Complete level goals (make the required sweets) to dig ever deeper. Watch the red line — if the cup overflows and a sweet gets stuck above it, the level is lost. Save yourself with boosters: bomb, shake, rainbow ball and plus-size.

Chain combos, unlock new levels, claim a reward every day, compete on the depth leaderboard. English and Russian supported, progress saved to the cloud.
