Merge Monsters is a cozy merge game with glossy cartoon heroes. Buy eggs with coins, drag matching monsters onto each other and discover new species — from tiny Sprout to the fiery Wyrm King. There are 16 species in the book, and every monster earns coins each second: the rarer the monster, the bigger the income. Complete level goals, claim rewards and climb higher. Stuck? Four boosters help: an upgrade gem, a wild joker star, a board clear and the Gust that shuffles the whole board. Watch a video to revive a full board or grab bonus coins. Progress and coins are saved to the cloud, and you can play as a guest.
