1. Tap a domino in your hand and it joins an open end of the chain with the matching pips.
2. Playable tiles glow. If a tile fits both ends, the game highlights them — tap the end you want.
3. Stuck? Tap the bazaar for a draw. Draw tokens are limited; once they run out, a draw costs a heart.
4. A double scores extra and grants a draw token. A clean streak multiplies your points.
5. Your goal is to place the required number of tiles. Three misses break the chain.
