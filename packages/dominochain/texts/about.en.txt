Domino Chain is a calm puzzle with a fairground heart. A ribbon of tiles grows across a polka-dot tablecloth: it has two open ends, and every new domino must match the pips on one of them. Playable tiles glow — tap one and the chain rolls on. A double scores extra and hands you a free draw token, while a clean streak multiplies your points. When nothing fits, pull a tile from the bazaar: tokens are limited, and tapping the wrong domino costs a heart. Twelve levels carry you from a ten-tile goal to a twenty-tile garland, with a fair stall offering three boosters along the way. Chase your best score without losing your hearts.
