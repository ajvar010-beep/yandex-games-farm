Steer the comet left and right with your mouse or finger — catch falling stars with the bowl. Yellow stars give points, golden ones give 5× more. Hearts restore a life, capsules trigger bonuses. Do NOT catch meteors — they burn a life! A no-miss streak grows the score multiplier up to ×5. Complete the level goal to climb higher. Spend stardust ✦ in the shop (the star button) on slow time, double dust and the shield.
