Tap the screen when a firefly flashes — only a glowing one can be caught. A catch brings points and combo; tapping a dark firefly scares it and resets the combo. Orange caterpillars crawl along the bottom of the meadow — a sting costs a life, and you have two. Catch the required number of fireflies to reach the next level. From level 5 catch the moon moth — it never fades and pays ×4 score. In the shop you can spend dust on a bubble (absorbs a sting), a lantern (longer glow), a net (bigger radius) and a brew (slower caterpillars).
