Brass Order is a calm sliding tile puzzle made by hand in a workshop: dark walnut, a brass frame with screws and cream tiles with engraved numbers. Tap a tile next to the empty slot to slide it and put the numbers in order. There are twelve boards: four at 3×3, four at 4×4 and four at 5×5. Each one has a par and a move limit — solve the board without running out of moves. Wins pay coins, and coins buy hints, undos and reshuffles. No timers, no energy, no rush — just you, the board and a little order.
