Each player gets six cards. The bottom card of the deck sets the trump suit.

To attack, tap a card in your hand and it goes to the table. You may add only ranks that are already on the table, and at most six attacks per round.

To defend, tap a card in your hand that can beat the open attack. Beat with a higher card of the same suit; a trump can only be beaten by a higher trump. If you cannot beat it, press Take.

Once every attack is beaten, press Done: the table goes to the discard pile and you become the attacker. If the defender takes the cards, you keep attacking.

After each round both players draw back up to six cards while the deck lasts. Whoever runs out of cards first wins.
