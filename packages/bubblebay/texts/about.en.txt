Bubble Bay is a cheerful underwater arcade about a brave crab and a sea of bubbles. Glass bubbles rise from the seabed: tap to pop them, collect shiny pearls ❁ and build long no-miss streaks — the longer the streak, the bigger your score multiplier, up to ×5! Keep your eyes open: sea mines float among the bubbles, and one touch costs a life. But do pop the rare jellyfish — it holds 15× more points!

Catch hearts to restore lives and bonus capsules: the sea breeze slows time down, while double pearls double your loot. Spend earned pearls in the shop on boosts: the breeze, the doubler, a protective net that blocks one mine, and a mighty tide that drags every bubble down — away from the dangerous shore.

Clear all 20 levels — bubbles rise faster and mines grow more numerous with each one. Out of lives? Bring the crab back by watching a video. Records and progress are saved, and with Yandex ID they live in the cloud. The game speaks Russian and English.
