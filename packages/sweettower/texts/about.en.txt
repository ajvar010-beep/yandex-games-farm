Sweet Tower is a cozy stacking arcade game about the art of baking. A new cake layer swings above the tower: tap at the right moment to drop it straight. The overhang is cut off, and the thinner the layer gets, the harder it is to land perfectly. A streak of perfect drops multiplies your score up to ×5. Keep your eyes open: sometimes a rare star layer appears — it holds 15× more points!

Each level awards sprinkles — the bakery currency. Spend them on a guide ruler, a calm pendulum, melting cream that widens your layer, or bouncy cream: with it layers are never cut off — a lifesaver on the fastest floors.

Complete all 20 levels, build a cake to the sky and seat the cherry on the very top. Simple one-tap controls, cute hand-drawn look, cloud saves and best scores — play on your phone or computer.
