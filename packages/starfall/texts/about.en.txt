Starfall is a cozy arcade about a comet catching falling stars. Steer the comet with your mouse or finger, collect yellow and golden stars, grab hearts and handy bonuses — and never touch burning meteors: they cost you a life! Watch for the rare supernova — it pays out 15 times more. The longer your no-miss streak, the higher the multiplier — up to ×5. Spend stardust in the shop on slow time, double dust, an umbrella shield and the star magnet that pulls drops closer. Clear all 20 levels to clean up the sky. The game saves automatically, and with Yandex ID your record is kept in the cloud on any device.
