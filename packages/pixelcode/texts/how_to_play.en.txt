1. The numbers above a column and beside a row show runs of filled cells.
2. Tap a cell to fill it. Switch to Cross mode to mark cells you know are empty.
3. Numbers go in order and need at least one empty cell between them.
4. A completed line is crossed out automatically.
5. Three mistakes per pattern: filling a cell that is not in the picture costs one try.
