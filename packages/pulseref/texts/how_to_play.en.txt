1. Open a stage from the Songs menu — the first stages are unlocked, the rest open as you win.
2. Tap a lane as its note touches the hit line. On a keyboard use D, F, J, K or the arrow keys.
3. Perfect timing pays more points and coins, and a streak of clean notes builds combo and multiplier.
4. Crossed diamonds are phantoms. Never tap them: your combo breaks and the pulse drops. Let them pass.
5. Keep the pulse bar alive and finish the song — the stage counts and the next one unlocks.
6. In the shop buy a shield, slow-mo and a coin multiplier; ads offer a second chance and double rewards.
